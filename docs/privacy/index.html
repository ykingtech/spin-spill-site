Privacy Policy

Our approach

Spin & Spill is designed as an offline party game. We do not require an account, and we do not collect, sell, or share personal information.

Information stored on your device

Player names, custom questions, game settings, and session scores are used only to run your game. They stay on your device unless you choose to share them yourself.

Internet, analytics, and advertising

The app is intended to work offline. It does not use third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics services.

Children

Spin & Spill does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Safe / Family mode is provided for age-appropriate group play, but parents or guardians should supervise use where appropriate.

Contact

For a privacy question, contact lankaknot@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
